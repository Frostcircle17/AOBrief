i Important Notice

O&A is an independent initiative founded by law students. Its activities are limited to research, drafting assistance, litigation-support research and educational or knowledge-sharing initiatives, subject to applicable professional rules and supervision where required. O&A is not a law firm and does not independently provide legal representation or hold itself out as a provider of regulated legal services. Matters requiring legal advice or representation should be taken to a qualified advocate.
